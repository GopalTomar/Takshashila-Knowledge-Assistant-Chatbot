    About this assistant

    What it knows

    The public Takshashila website — publications, blogs, op-eds listed under “In the news”, team profiles, research areas, courses and programmes — refreshed automatically every day at 06:00 IST. Staff with an access token can also query the internal Commit knowledge base.

    How answers are checked

    Answers are generated only from retrieved passages. Every citation is verified against the passage it points to; unsupported citations are removed, and when the evidence is insufficient the assistant says so instead of guessing.

    Tips

    • Name a person to find their work: “What has Pranay Kotasthane written on semiconductors?”
    • Use a title to find a specific publication.
    • Choose Find documents to list matching documents without an AI answer.
